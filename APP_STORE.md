## App Store Description

### Short Description
Run AI chatbots privately on your iPhone and iPad. No internet, no subscriptions, no data collection.

### Full Description

Local LLM brings the power of large language models directly to your device. Chat with AI models that run 100% on your iPhone or iPad - no internet connection required, no subscriptions, and absolutely no data leaves your device.

**Why Local LLM?**

- PRIVACY FIRST: All AI processing happens on your device. Your conversations never leave your phone.
- NO INTERNET REQUIRED: Once you download a model, chat works completely offline.
- NO SUBSCRIPTIONS: Download once, use forever. No monthly fees or token limits.
- 14+ MODELS: Choose from a curated selection of open-source models optimized for mobile.

**Supported Models:**
- Meta Llama 3.1, 3.2 (3B-8B)
- Mistral 7B Instruct
- Microsoft Phi-2, Phi-3 Mini
- Google Gemma 2
- Qwen 2.5 (including Coder variant)
- DeepSeek R1 Distill
- TinyLlama, StableLM, Yi
- And more...

**Features:**
- Streaming responses - see answers generate in real-time
- Conversation history - pick up where you left off
- Customizable - adjust temperature, system prompts, and more
- Beautiful themes - light, dark, and custom accent colors
- Universal - optimized for both iPhone and iPad

**Recommended devices:**
- iPhone 12 or newer (1-3B models)
- iPhone 15 Pro or newer (7B+ models)
- iPad with M-series chip (best experience)

## App Review Notes

- The app downloads AI model files (GGUF format) from HuggingFace.co
- All AI inference runs on-device using the llama.cpp engine with Metal GPU acceleration
- No user data is collected or transmitted
- The app requires iOS 17.0 or later
- Models range from 670 MB to 4.9 GB in size
- Larger models (7B+) require devices with 8+ GB RAM
